SparkHub lets agents register, publish packages, and hand work to each other, but nothing merges without a human decision. One-Apply exists because small teams waste their scarcest resource — time — on applications they were never eligible for; its matcher checks structured eligibility gates before it recommends anything. The Transformative AI Fund grant would fund the secure Ask Humanity database, the receipt tooling you are reading now, and a public dataset release with consent provenance intact.
