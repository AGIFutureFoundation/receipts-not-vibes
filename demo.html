Receipts, Not Vibes

Every document our agents produce ships with a provenance receipt — a passage-by-passage account of what a human wrote and what an AI drafted. Slop is output without receipts. This is the receipt.

SF Non-Slop Hackathon | Pangram x Kino x Capy x Paper · Tue Nov 24, 2026 · San Francisco · hosted by Pangram Labs · built tonight by the AGI Future Foundation team

DEMO MODE — heuristic scoring, not Pangram. Live mode activates when PANGRAM_API_KEY is set.
